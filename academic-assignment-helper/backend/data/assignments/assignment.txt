Title: Transformer Models in Natural Language Processing

Transformers have revolutionized NLP by introducing self-attention mechanisms that allow models to capture long-range dependencies in text. Key techniques include pretraining on large corpora and fine-tuning for downstream tasks. Recent advances such as BERT, GPT, and RoBERTa demonstrate significant improvements in performance across a wide range of NLP benchmarks. Additionally, efficiency optimizations like knowledge distillation and quantization enable faster inference without sacrificing much accuracy.

Applications of transformers span machine translation, question answering, text summarization, and more. Future research may explore cross-lingual transfer, multimodal integration, and adaptation to low-resource domains.
